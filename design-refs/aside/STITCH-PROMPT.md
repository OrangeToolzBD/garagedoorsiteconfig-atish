# Stitch prompt — inner-page sidebar

Paste the block below into Stitch. Ask for **desktop and mobile** of each.

---

Design **only the sidebar column** of an interior article page for a local
garage-door repair company's website. This is the narrow column that sits
beside the article text. **Do not design the navigation, the page header,
the article body, or the footer. Output only this one column.**

Give me **8 clearly different sidebar designs**, each shown at **desktop
(a 320px-wide column)** and **mobile (390px wide, where it sits above or
below the article as a full-width block)**. They must differ in *structure*
— what modules they contain and how those are arranged — not just in colour
or corner radius. Number them 1 to 8.

## The content that exists

Each sidebar is built from one to three of these modules. Mix them
differently in each design.

- **Contact card** — a small icon, a heading like *"Get a free quote"*, one
  sentence like *"Most Dallas repairs are done on the first visit."*, a
  primary button reading *"Request a Quote"*, and one secondary text link
  such as *"Service Areas"* or *"Read The Guides"*.
- **On this page** — a list of 3 to 6 links to headings within the article,
  e.g. *"What usually breaks"*, *"What a repair costs"*, *"When to skip DIY"*.
- **Services list** — up to 5 service links plus an *"All Services"* link.
- **Service areas** — up to 8 nearby town or neighbourhood links.
- **Reasons to choose us** — exactly 4 short items, each an outline icon plus
  three or four words. No numbers in them.
- **City label** — small text such as *"Dallas, TX"*.

At least **3 of the 8** must be a single module only. At least **2** must
stack three modules.

## Banned — we do not have this data, do not invent it

Star ratings, review counts, testimonials, quotes from customers, "trusted
by" logos, awards, certification or licence badges, prices, discounts,
countdown timers, opening hours, dates, staff names or photographs, a map,
a newsletter or email signup, a contact form with input fields, social
media icons, chat bubbles, "X people viewed this", and **any statistic or
number** — no "500+ jobs", no "24/7", no "15 years".

A phone number appears on fewer than 1% of these sites. **Do not build any
design around a phone number.** At most one design may show one, and it
must still look correct with that line removed entirely.

## Rules

- The column is **320px** on desktop. Nothing may overflow it.
- On mobile it becomes a full-width block. Say for each design whether it
  should sit above or below the article on a phone.
- The sidebar sticks to the top of the screen as the reader scrolls, so
  keep it **short** — under about 520px tall on desktop. If a design is
  taller than that, say so and it will not stick.
- Buttons at least 44px tall.
- Assume a **light page background**. One or two designs may use a dark or
  coloured card — say which.
- Link lists must survive long labels like *"Buckner Terrace / Everglade
  Park"* without breaking the column.
- Show real placeholder text, not "Lorem ipsum".

## What I want back

For each of the 8: the desktop column, the mobile block, which modules it
uses, and one line saying what makes it structurally different.
