# Stitch prompt — "service areas" section

Paste the block below into Stitch. Ask for **desktop and mobile** of each.

---

Design **only the "areas we cover" section** of a local garage-door repair
company's home page. It is one horizontal band in the middle of a long page.
**Do not design the navigation, the page header, any other section, or the
footer. Output only this one band.**

Give me **8 clearly different designs**, each at **desktop (1280px)** and
**mobile (390px)**. They must differ in *structure* — how the place names are
arranged and what carries them — not just in colour or corner radius. Number
them 1 to 8.

## The content that exists

- **A short eyebrow line** above the heading, e.g. *"Around Dallas"*.
- **A heading**, e.g. *"Areas We Cover"*.
- **One lead sentence**, e.g. *"We run out of Dallas and cover the
  neighborhoods close to the center first."*
- **A list of place names.** Neighbourhoods and nearby towns. Names run from
  short (*"Kessler"*) to long (*"Buckner Terrace / Everglade Park"*).
- **Optionally a distance beside each name**, written like *"1 mi"*, *"14 mi"*.
  Some sites have these, some do not. At least **3 of the 8** designs must look
  right with **no distances at all**.
- **A "View all areas" link.**
- **Optionally one closing sentence** below the list.

## The hard constraint — the list length varies enormously

The same design must work with **3 place names** and with **26**. Most sites
today have 3; the largest has 26. Show each design **twice at desktop**: once
with 3 names and once with 26. A design that looks empty at 3, or becomes an
unreadable wall at 26, is not usable.

On a phone, 26 names at 44px each is over a thousand pixels of list. At least
**half** the designs must have a genuine answer to that — columns, a compact
inline flow, or grouping — not just one long stack.

## Some names are not links

A few places have no page yet, so they render as **plain text, not links**.
Every design must make an unlinked name look deliberate rather than broken.
Show at least one unlinked name in each design.

## Banned — we do not have this data, do not invent it

A map of any kind, map pins, location markers, ZIP or postcodes, drive times
("25 min"), addresses, population figures, "50+ areas served" or any other
count, star ratings, reviews, testimonials, photographs of places or
neighbourhoods, flags, coordinates, a search or filter box, an "expand" that
needs JavaScript, and a phone number.

**No numbers other than the mile distances described above.**

## Rules

- Assume a **light page background**. One or two designs may use a tinted or
  dark band — say which.
- Tap targets at least 44px on mobile. No horizontal scrolling at 390px.
- Long names must not overflow or be cut off.
- Keep the band a sensible height — under about 560px at desktop with 26
  names, under 700px on mobile.
- Show real placeholder names, not "Lorem ipsum". Use these:
  Old East Dallas, South Dallas, Kessler, Lakewood, Lower Greenville,
  Northeast Dallas, Cedar Crest, Buckner Terrace / Everglade Park,
  Downtown Historic District, Balch Springs, DeSoto, Ferris.

## What I want back

For each of the 8: desktop with 3 names, desktop with 26 names, the mobile
layout, and one line saying what makes it structurally different.
