Austin, TX Garage Door Maintenance Guide

Regular maintenance of your garage door system is crucial for longevity and safety in the Austin, TX climate. The fluctuating temperatures can cause metal components to expand and contract, leading to premature wear if not properly lubricated and adjusted.

One of the most critical components of your garage door system is the torsion spring. These springs bear the immense weight of the door, making it feel light enough to lift with one hand or a small electric motor.

How often should I lubricate the tracks? expand_more
It's recommended to lubricate the tracks and rollers at least twice a year. Use a silicone-based lubricant, avoiding heavy greases that attract dirt.
Why is my garage door making a grinding noise? expand_more
Grinding noises usually indicate worn rollers, a lack of lubrication, or hinges that are starting to fail. It's best to have a professional inspect it to prevent further damage.
Can I paint my garage door myself? expand_more
Yes, but proper preparation is key. Ensure the surface is clean, lightly sanded, and use a high-quality exterior latex paint suitable for the door's material (steel, wood, or aluminum).

When these springs fail, it's often sudden and loud. Many homeowners mistake the sound for something falling in the garage or even a localized structural issue.
