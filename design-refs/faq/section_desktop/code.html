Before you call

Questions we get a lot

Is replacing a spring a DIY job? expand_more

No. Garage door springs are under extreme tension. Attempting a DIY replacement without proper tools and training can lead to serious injury or damage to the door system.

Why is my door making a grinding noise? expand_more

Grinding usually indicates worn rollers or lack of lubrication. If left unaddressed, this creates friction that can burn out your opener motor prematurely.

Do you offer same-day repairs? expand_more

We stock our trucks with common high-cycle springs and hardware to ensure most repairs are completed in a single visit, often on the day you call.

How often should I service my door? expand_more

We recommend a professional safety check and lubrication once a year to prevent major hardware failures and keep your warranty valid.
