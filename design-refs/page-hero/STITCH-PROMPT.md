# Stitch prompt — inner-page hero

Paste the block below into Stitch. Ask for **desktop and mobile** of each.

---

Design **only a page-header band** for an interior page of a local
garage-door repair company's website. This is the strip that sits directly
under the site navigation and above the article text. **Do not design the
navigation bar, the article, the sidebar, the footer, or any other section.
Output only this one band.**

Give me **8 clearly different layouts**, each shown at **desktop (1280px)**
and **mobile (390px)**. They must differ in *structure and arrangement* —
not just colour or font size. Number them 1 to 8.

## The only content that exists

Use these and nothing else:

- **A breadcrumb trail**: `Home › Services › <page name>` (two or three
  levels; some pages have only two).
- **One page title (H1)**. Assume it is **long**: 30 to 75 characters,
  typically around 46. Example: *"Garage Door Repair for Dallas Homes Built
  1973-2007"*. It must not be truncated or clipped at any width.
- **One short description sentence**, about 120-160 characters. Example:
  *"Broken springs and stuck tracks are common in Dallas homes built
  1973-2007. See what a repair call involves and when to skip DIY fixes."*
  Optional — at least 3 of the 8 layouts should work with **no** description.
- **City and state** as a small label, e.g. *"Dallas, TX"*.
- **One photograph** of a garage door or a technician at work. Optional —
  at least 4 of the 8 layouts must work with **no photo at all**.

## Banned — we do not have this data, do not invent it

Star ratings, review counts, testimonials, "trusted by" logos, awards or
certification badges, a phone number, prices, opening hours, dates, author
names, read time, share buttons, tags or categories, search boxes, social
icons, maps, progress bars, a "back" arrow, and any second call-to-action
button. **No numbers or statistics of any kind.**

A call-to-action button is allowed in at most 2 of the 8 layouts, and it
must be a single button reading "Request a Quote".

## Rules

- The breadcrumb must stay readable and must not wrap into a messy stack on
  mobile. On mobile it may shorten to `Home › Services` (dropping the last
  item).
- Assume a **light page background**. One or two layouts may use a dark or
  coloured band instead — say which.
- Everything must survive a **very long title** and a **very short title**.
- No horizontal scrolling at 390px. Tap targets at least 44px.
- Keep the band reasonably short — this is a header, not a hero. Under
  roughly 260px tall on desktop, under 320px on mobile, unless a photo
  layout genuinely needs more.
- Show real placeholder text, not "Lorem ipsum".

## What I want back

For each of the 8: the desktop layout, the mobile layout, and one line
saying what makes it structurally different from the others.
