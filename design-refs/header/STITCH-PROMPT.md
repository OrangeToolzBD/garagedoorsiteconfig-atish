# Stitch prompt — site header

Paste the block below into Stitch. Ask for **desktop and mobile** of each.

---

Design **only the header** of a local garage-door repair company's website —
the bar at the very top of every page, above all content. **Do not design the
hero, any page section, or the footer. Output only the header.**

Give me **8 clearly different designs**, each at **desktop (1280px)** and
**mobile (390px)**. They must differ in *structure and arrangement* — where
each piece sits, what is grouped with what — not just colour or corner radius.
Number them 1 to 8.

## Every design must contain all of this

1. **A thin announcement strip across the very top.** Two short lines of text:
   *"Serving Dallas & the surrounding metro"* and *"Same-day service
   available"*. This strip is required in all 8 — it may be styled, split,
   aligned or coloured differently, but it cannot be removed.
2. **The company lockup**: a small square logo mark, the company name
   *"Dallas Garage Door Co"*, and a tagline beneath it, *"Garage Door Repair &
   Installation"*.
3. **Navigation with three dropdown menus** — *Services*, *Service Areas*,
   *Guides* — plus two plain links, *About* and *Contact*.
4. **One button**: *"Free Quote"*.

Show at least one dropdown **open** in each desktop design. A dropdown holds a
list of links: Services has about 6, Service Areas up to 18, Guides about 4.
Each opens with an "All Services" / "All Service Areas" link first.

## Mobile

On a phone the navigation collapses behind a **hamburger button** into a
**drawer**. Show each design twice on mobile: **closed**, and **open with the
drawer showing**. The drawer must scroll if it is taller than the screen.

## Banned — we do not have this data, do not invent it

A search box, a shopping cart, a login or account link, a language or currency
switcher, star ratings, review counts, "trusted by" logos, certification or
licence badges, social media icons, live chat, a "book online" calendar, an
emergency banner with a countdown, opening hours, an email address, a street
address, and **any number or statistic** — no "24/7", no "500+ jobs", no "15
years".

**A phone number appears on fewer than 1% of these sites.** Do not build any
design around one. At most two designs may show one, and each must still look
correct with that element removed entirely.

## Rules

- The header **sticks to the top of the screen** while the page scrolls. Say
  for each design what stays stuck — the whole thing, or only part of it.
- Keep the sticky part **under about 90px tall on desktop and 60px on mobile**.
  The announcement strip may scroll away rather than stick.
- The company name can be long — *"Punta Gorda Garage Door Pros"* — and must
  not be truncated or overlap the navigation.
- The Service Areas dropdown holds up to 18 links, some as long as *"Buckner
  Terrace / Everglade Park"*. It must not run off the screen.
- Tap targets at least 44px on mobile.
- Assume a **light page background**. One or two designs may use a dark or
  coloured bar — say which.
- Show real placeholder text, not "Lorem ipsum".

## What I want back

For each of the 8: the desktop header with a dropdown open, the mobile header
closed, the mobile drawer open, and one line saying what makes it structurally
different from the others.
